# Mindwire

**One agent. Bring your subscriptions.**

Mindwire is your personal agent for everyday work and coding. Bring your supported AI
subscriptions, connect your computer, and put it to work from your iPhone, iPad, or browser.

- **Get everyday work done.** Organize files, process data, prepare documents, and automate repetitive tasks.
- **Build from anywhere.** Write and review code, manage projects, and use a full terminal.
- **Keep work moving.** Start a task, close the app, and come back to the result while your computer stays online.
- **Stay in control.** Follow progress, answer questions, and approve actions along the way.
- **Work where you want.** Use your computer, a server, or a cloud workspace.
